WEB INI BERFOKUS PADA KUSTOMISASI.

STYLE:
Design the web UI with a strong Apple-style aesthetic.

Principles to follow strictly:
- Minimalist and calm interface, no visual noise
- Generous spacing and clear visual hierarchy
- Neutral dark or light color palette, no saturated colors
- Subtle shadows, soft borders, no harsh outlines
- Typography-focused layout, text is the hero
- Use system-like sans serif fonts similar to SF Pro
- Icons must be thin, simple, and consistent in size
- Avoid heavy gradients, avoid glassmorphism excess, avoid flashy animations

Layout behavior:
- Content-first design, UI should disappear and let content breathe
- Smooth and subtle transitions only, no dramatic motion
- Buttons should look quiet and confident, not loud
- Navigation should feel native, simple, and predictable

Overall feeling:
- Calm
- Premium
- Focused
- Effortless
- Apple-like but not a clone

Do not add unnecessary components.
Do not add decorative elements without purpose.
Do not over-design.
